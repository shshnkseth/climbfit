How It Works

LED Holds. Cloud Routes.
Solid Hardware.

Every hold lights up in color to show you the route. Connects over Bluetooth. Pick a problem in the app and go.

💡

Color-Coded LED Holds

Every hold has an RGB LED behind it. The colors tell you what to grab: Green for start, Cyan for hands & feet, Orange for feet only, and Pink for the finish. No guessing.

Why It Works

One Wall. Thousands of Routes.

Traditional climbing walls need tall ceilings, professional route setters, and constant resets. ClimbFit gives you thousands of routes on a single wall — change them instantly from the app, no bolts involved.

01

Fits Normal Rooms

Works under standard 10–12 ft ceilings. No major construction needed. Mount it flat on a wall or set it up freestanding.

02

No Route-Setting Bills

Switch routes, sequences, and drills instantly from the app. No more paying someone to unbolt and rearrange holds by hand.

03

Easy Class Management

Run PE classes, team practices, or group sessions using color-coded difficulty levels. Works from any tablet.

04

Shared Route Library

Access routes created by climbers and setters around the world. Try the same problems your friends are climbing on their walls.

05

Track Your Progress

See which routes you've sent, how fast you climbed them, and how your strength is improving over weeks and months.

06

Low Maintenance

Tough hardware that handles daily use. Firmware updates happen over the air. Holds are sweat-resistant. Very little upkeep.
