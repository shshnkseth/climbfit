Turnkey Process

From Measuring Your Room to Your First Climb, We Take Care of Everything

You don't need architects or climbing contractors. Our engineering team manages every step from site survey to teacher onboarding.

01 📐

We Measure Your Room

We assess your room dimensions, ceiling clearance, wall structure, and electrical points to plan the best layout.

02 ✏️

Design a 3D Layout

We create a customized 3D plan showing the board or wall in your room, ensuring safe fall zones and clear walkways.

03 🛠️

Install Everything

Our technicians erect the structural steel frame, CNC birch panels, illuminated hold matrix, and wiring cleanly.

04 🛡️

Lay Down Safety Mats

We install custom-cut, dual-layer high-density crash mats across the entire landing zone with zero seam gaps.

05 📱

App & Bluetooth Setup

We pair your wall with the ClimbFit app, calibrate all 1,000+ routes, activate game modes, and test every LED.

06 🎓

Train Your Team

We run a hands-on session for your gym coaches or PE teachers on setting up drills, running games, and supervising.
