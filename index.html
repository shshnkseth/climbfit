How ClimbFit Works

Climb, Switch, Play.
Zero Physical Resetting.

Our mobile app controls lights within the climbing holds, making it easy to create varied challenges for different age groups and skill levels on the exact same wall.

  1. 01

    Choose an Activity

    Pick an activity, route, or game directly from the ClimbFit smartphone app. Filter by grade, setter, or specific athletic movement.

  2. 02

    Follow the Lights

    Holds illuminate with precise, high-visibility colors instantly (<30ms), guiding climbers step-by-step from start to finish hold.

  3. 03

    Change the Challenge

    Select a brand-new activity or next route with a single tap. No wrenching holds, no manual route resetting, and zero downtime.

01 📐

Site Inspection & Space Evaluation

Phase 01 · Planning

We evaluate room dimensions, ceiling clearance, structural load capacity, and fall zone boundaries to verify space readiness and safe clearance.

02 ✏️

Design & Customization

Phase 02 · Engineering

We generate detailed 3D layouts and CAD drawings, customizing wall dimensions (Size S, M, L or custom geometry) and frame fitment to your room.

03 🛠️

Wall & Hardware Assembly

Phase 03 · Installation

Our team installs the engineered steel chassis, CNC-drilled BWP grade plywood panels, and sealed RGB LED hold modules with secure cable routing.

04 🛡️

Custom-Fitted Impact Matting

Phase 04 · Safety

We fit dual-layer high-density crash mats tailored seamlessly to the exact wall footprint to ensure certified landing absorption with zero gap slippage.

05 📱

App Setup & Calibration

Phase 05 · Software

We pair the Bluetooth/Wi-Fi wall controller (<30ms latency), test all diode channels, and configure the local route library and interactive game modes.

06 🎓

Coach & Staff Onboarding

Phase 06 · Handover

We run a hands-on training workshop for coaches, PE teachers, and facility staff on session management, game rules, and basic maintenance.
