How It Works

Three Steps to
Smarter Training

01

Load a Session

Choose from a library of pre-built routes or create a custom sequence in the ClimbFit app. Set difficulty, target holds, and timing goals.

02

Climb & React

Holds illuminate in sequence as the climber ascends. Sensors detect each touch and register response time, force, and path accuracy instantly.

03

Review & Improve

Post-session analytics show movement heatmaps, reaction times, completion rates, and strength asymmetries — all exportable for coaching review.
