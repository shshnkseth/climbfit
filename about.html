Our Story

Bringing Climbing into the Digital Age

Climbing is one of the most natural, healthy ways to build whole-body strength and confidence. But traditional climbing walls take up huge spaces and are difficult to update.

We created ClimbFit to make climbing accessible to any facility. By putting smart lights inside durable climbing holds and connecting them to an intuitive app, one wall offers thousands of climbs.

Today, ClimbFit powers physical education in schools, training zones in commercial gyms, and active recreation centers—helping climbers of every age stay active.
