Origin Story

From Bengaluru's Bouldering Crags to Modern Smart Walls.

ClimbFit was started in Bengaluru by a collective of climbers, hardware architects, and sports scientists who witnessed the explosive growth of sport climbing in India after the Tokyo and Paris Olympics. While Indian climbers possessed immense grit, they lacked access to standardized, data-driven training tools.

We designed our first prototype in Indiranagar, testing custom dual-texture holds, moisture-sealed RGB LED controllers, and a bluetooth-synced route builder. The result was a rock-solid, responsive board that matched the precision of international training boards at a fraction of the landed import cost.

Today, ClimbFit is powering commercial climbing gyms, boutique fitness clubs, and premier institutions across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune, and beyond.
