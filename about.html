The Problem We Solved

One Wall. Thousands of Routes.

Traditional climbing walls are static. Want a new route? Someone has to unscrew every hold by hand and bolt them back in new positions. It takes hours, sometimes days. Most facilities just don't bother.

We took a different approach. Our holds have lights inside them. Open the app on your phone or tablet, pick a route, and the right holds glow instantly. A beginner warm-up, a kids' game, a serious training problem — all on the same wall, all in seconds.

Our Story

We Love Climbing. But We Couldn't Find a Gym.

Climbing is one of the most natural, healthy ways to move. It builds whole-body strength, grip endurance, balance, and focus. But if you live in most Indian cities, you know the frustration: there simply isn't a climbing gym around. The few that exist are often hours away in traffic, or don't fit into busy daily routines.

Building a traditional commercial climbing gym requires 20-foot ceilings, heavy structural modifications, and constant manual route setting that almost nobody has the space or budget to manage. We loved climbing and didn't want to give it up just because there wasn't a mega-gym down the street.

So we built what we wished existed: a compact, heavy-duty training board with illuminated LED holds that fits under standard 10-foot ceilings. Open the app on your phone, pick a climb, follow the glowing lights, and train anytime you want. Today, ClimbFit brings smart climbing boards, traverse walls, and complete turnkey setups to schools, fitness clubs, and private training spaces nationwide.
