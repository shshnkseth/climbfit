Founding Story

From a Gym Floor in Bangalore to Nationwide.

ClimbFit was founded in 2023 by a team of sports scientists, hardware engineers, and product designers who kept asking the same question: why does my smartwatch track every step I take, but the climbing wall in my gym has zero data?

We started with a single prototype wall in an Indiranagar gym, a handful of holds, and some very generous early users who let us drill sensors into their holds. Within three months, we had data that clearly showed reactive climbing improved cognitive performance by over 20% in adult athletes. We knew we were onto something.

Today, ClimbFit walls are in gyms and schools across Bangalore, Mumbai, and Hyderabad — and we're just getting started.
