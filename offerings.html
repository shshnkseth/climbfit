Turnkey Process

Turnkey Delivery in 6 Simple Stages

From the initial 3D site scan to coach certification, we handle every step of your climbing wall installation.

01 📐

Site Inspection

We evaluate your room dimensions, ceiling height, structural load capacity, and floor landing boundaries.

02 ✏️

3D Design & Layout

Our engineers draft custom 3D blueprints tailored to your room—wall-mounted structural framing or freestanding.

03 🛠️

Hardware Installation

Technicians assemble the engineered steel framework, precision timber panels, LED harness, and controllers.

04 🛡️

Custom Safety Matting

We fabricate and fit dual-layer high-density crash mats cut to the exact perimeter of your landing zone.

05 📱

App Setup & Sync

We configure the tablet controller, pair Bluetooth and Wi-Fi mesh, calibrate hold illumination, and test offline mode.

06 🎓

Staff & Coach Onboarding

Hands-on training for PE teachers and gym staff on game modes, route selection, and safety best practices.
