Core Product Architecture

ClimbFit Training Board

A modular climbing system combining precision-engineered timber panels, sealed RGB LED holds, and mobile app route illumination. Explore thousands of routes without moving a single physical hold.

● RGB Smart Hold Matrix | Engineered Steel Chassis
Smart LED Infrastructure

High-Density Route Variety on a Single Footprint

Traditional climbing walls demand days of manual wrenching, route resetting, and lost facility hours. With the ClimbFit Training Board, climbers simply open the iOS/Android app, select from thousands of standardized problems, and follow the illuminated holds.

  • ✓ Engineered Steel Frame: Wall-mounted or freestanding self-supporting configurations subject to site clearance.
  • ✓ RGB LED Smart Matrix: Sealed, impact-resistant holds with individual addressable color paths.
  • ✓ Compatible Hold Sets: Ergonomically contoured holds for all grip styles (crimps, slopers, pinches, jugs).
  • ✓ Dual-Layer Crash Matting: High-density impact-absorbing foam custom-fitted to your landing zone.

End-to-End Implementation

Turnkey Solutions — 6 Stages

From the initial architectural assessment to faculty onboarding, ClimbFit handles the complete end-to-end delivery lifecycle.

01 📐

Site Inspection

Our engineers perform a comprehensive spatial scan of your room: checking ceiling clearance, structural load capacity, power accessibility, and fall-zone clearance.

02 ✏️

Design & Customization

We finalize the 3D wall configuration, dimensions, and frame layout—customizing between wall-mounted structural anchors or freestanding self-supporting trusses.

03 🛠️

Wall & Hardware Assembly

Certified installation technicians assemble the engineered steel framework, precision timber panels, LED hold matrix wiring, and low-voltage control hardware.

04 🛡️

Custom-Fitted Matting

We fabricate and position certified dual-layer high-density impact crash mats, seamlessly tailored to the exact floor contours of your approved landing perimeter.

05 📱

App Setup & Calibration

We configure the ClimbFit control suite on institutional tablets/smartphones, pair Bluetooth Low Energy mesh, calibrate hold illumination, and verify offline mode.

06 🎓

Coach & Staff Onboarding

Hands-on operational training for PE teachers and sports directors: introducing game modes, route selection, student activity logging, and safety protocols.
