How We Build It

Turnkey Delivery in 6 Simple Steps

From measuring your space to your first climb, our team takes care of everything.

01 📐

Site Survey

We check your room dimensions, ceiling height, wall structure, and floor space to ensure a safe fit.

02 ✏️

3D Plan

We design a 3D layout customized to your room, choosing wall-mounted or freestanding frames.

03 🛠️

Installation

Our technicians install the steel frame, wood panels, glowing hold matrix, and wiring cleanly.

04 🛡️

Safety Mats

We fit custom-cut, dual-layer high-density crash mats across the landing area for maximum safety.

05 📱

App Setup

We set up your tablet or phone, connect the wall via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi, and test the lighting.

06 🎓

Staff Training

We train your PE teachers or gym staff on how to pick routes, start games, and supervise safely.
