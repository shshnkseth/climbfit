Training Board

The ClimbFit Training Board

A climbing wall where the holds light up. Pick a route on your phone, the holds glow, and you start climbing.

● RGB LED Holds | Solid Timber Panels

How It Works

On a regular wall, changing routes means pulling holds off and screwing new ones on — it takes hours. With ClimbFit, the holds stay put. The lights change the route for you, instantly.

📱

1,000+ Training Routes & Daily Updates

Browse 1,000+ climbs graded V0–V14, with fresh routes added daily by coaches and climbers. Plus interactive games like Memory, Relay, and Multiplayer.

💡

Follow the Lights

Each hold has a built-in LED. It shows you where to start, where your hands and feet go, and where to finish.

How We Set It Up

6 Steps. We Do All of Them.

From measuring your room to your first climb — we take care of everything.

01 📐

We Measure Your Room

We check dimensions, ceiling height, wall structure, and floor space to make sure everything fits safely.

02 ✏️

Design a Layout

We create a 3D plan for your room — wall-mounted or freestanding, sized to fit your space.

03 🛠️

Install Everything

Our team puts up the steel frame, timber panels, LED holds, and all the wiring. Clean and solid.

04 🛡️

Lay Down Safety Mats

We fit custom-cut, dual-layer crash mats across the full landing area. No gaps.

05 📱

Set Up the App

We connect your tablet or phone to the wall via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi and test every light.

06 🎓

Train Your Team

We show your PE teachers or gym staff how to pick routes, start games, and supervise safely.
