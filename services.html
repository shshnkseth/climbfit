On a regular wall, changing routes means pulling holds off and screwing new ones on — it takes hours. With ClimbFit, the holds stay put. The lights change the route for you, instantly.

Traverse Wall

Traverse Walls — Climb Sideways

A traverse wall means you climb sideways, not up. It fits hallways and standard ceilings, and lots of kids can climb at once.

Sideways Climbing · Fits Any Room

Many Climbers at Once

No waiting in line. 4 to 8+ students can climb side-by-side along the wall at the same time.

Fits Standard 8–10 ft Ceilings

Because you climb sideways instead of up, traverse walls work in regular rooms, hallways, and activity areas.

Modular Length (12 ft to 48+ ft)
